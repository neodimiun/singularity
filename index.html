Zoom & pan

Zooming: drag across a chart to zoom to that span; use the buttons; or focus a chart (click it or Tab to it) and press + / − to zoom, ← / → to pan and 0 to reset. Once a chart is focused the mouse wheel zooms too (Ctrl + wheel or a trackpad pinch works at any time). On touch screens, pinch to zoom, swipe sideways to pan and tap a point for details. The narrowest view is 7 days. Double-click resets.
